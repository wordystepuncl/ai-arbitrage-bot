THE PARALLAX PRINCIPLE

A price is just one perspective.

We request public spot bid and ask prices from Binance, OKX and Gate in parallel. Every pair uses USDT. The scanner evaluates all six directed routes using the ask to buy and the bid to sell.

quantity = capital / (ask × (1 + buy fee))
net = quantity × bid × (1 − sell fee)
× (1 − slippage) − transfer − capital

X-ray reveals response times and quote age. A green result is a mathematical estimate, not an executable promise. This tool does not use a trained AI model, submit orders or connect wallets.
